04 · Architecture

Small core. Clear layers.

LiBot / Li is designed as a set of smaller, separable layers. Each has a clear responsibility and can be tested or replaced without destabilising the whole.

LAYER 01

Communication

Interface between people, devices and communication environments.

LAYER 02

Session

Maintains the logic and current state of an active communication session.

LAYER 03

Policy

Rules, permissions and safe execution of requested actions.

LAYER 04

Intelligence

Context, caller intelligence, language and AI capabilities.

LAYER 05

Voice

Realtime voice communication and audio-session handling.

LAYER 06

Devices

Mobile and external communication environments separated from core logic.
