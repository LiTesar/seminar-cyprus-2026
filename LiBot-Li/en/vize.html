01 · Vision

Personal communication. Intelligent.

LiBot / Li is being developed as an intelligent layer above everyday communication — working with voice, context, people, devices and communication rules rather than acting as an isolated chatbot.

VISION 01

One logical conversation

Communication is treated as one logical session rather than a chain of disconnected application steps.

VISION 02

Context before action

Understand the situation and rules first. Select the technical communication path second.

VISION 03

Human remains human

AI should extend communication and reduce friction while keeping people in control.
